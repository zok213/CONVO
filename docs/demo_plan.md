```
VISUAL STYLE: Cinematic documentary realism. Color grade: warm teal-orange film look — deep shadows, slightly desaturated midtones, warm highlights. Consistent throughout all clips. No cartoon, no illustration, no CGI. Photorealistic only.

FILM STYLE: 24fps cinematic. Subtle natural camera motion — slight handheld drift for outdoor scenes, locked off for UI/product close-ups. Lens flare permitted in outdoor golden-hour shots. No rapid cuts or shaky cam.

MAIN CHARACTER A — THE WORKER:
Vietnamese male, early 30s, 170cm, lean build. Weathered face, calm and focused expression. Short black hair under a scuffed white hard hat. Faded orange safety vest with yellow reflective stripes. Dark navy work trousers. Brown leather work gloves. A bone-conduction earphone (titanium-grey, curved arc shape) worn on his right cheekbone in scenes where it appears. A black smartphone in a chest-mounted holder clipped to his vest.

MAIN CHARACTER B — THE SUPERVISOR:
Korean male, early 50s, heavier build. Weathered but authoritative face. Short silver-black hair under a yellow hard hat. Grey reflective vest. Khaki trousers. A bone-conduction earphone (same titanium-grey model as Character A) worn on his right cheekbone in scenes where it appears. Clipboard in hand.

MAIN CHARACTER C — THE HEARING-IMPAIRED WOMAN:
Young Asian woman, late 20s, professional appearance. Neat black hair, shoulder length. Light-colored business blouse. A small skin-toned behind-the-ear hearing aid on her left ear. A bone-conduction earphone (same titanium-grey model) on her left cheekbone in scenes where it appears. A smartphone lying flat on the table in front of her in office scenes.

ENVIRONMENT A — DA NANG PORT:
Vietnam. Busy working port. Golden-hour or mid-morning natural light. Blue sky with haze from sea air. Red and yellow gantry cranes. Blue and orange stacked shipping containers. Worn concrete dock surface. Forklifts and workers visible in background. Slightly humid atmosphere.

ENVIRONMENT B — OFFICE:
Modern, warm. Large windows with natural daylight. Polished wood conference table. Mixed-nationality colleagues. Plants visible. Clean, professional, not sterile.

THE APP UI (whenever smartphone screen is shown):
Dark navy background. App name "WaveLens Lite" in white at top. Green glowing circular waveform in center. Vietnamese text in white above, English text in lighter grey below. Subtle purple-to-teal gradient accents. Simple, clean, no clutter.

THE EARPHONE (whenever shown):
Titanium-grey bone-conduction earphone. Thin curved arc. Two flat rectangular transducer pads at each end. Minimal branding. Premium, industrial-grade feel.
```

## RECORDING CHECKLIST — For the Live Screen Demo Section

Before recording the app demo portion:

- [ ] Open the app in mobile Chrome (Android preferred for Bluetooth reliability)
- [ ] Pair Bluetooth earphone before opening the browser
- [ ] Set phone to Do Not Disturb (no call interruptions during recording)
- [ ] Use screen recording with internal audio capture enabled
- [ ] Have Vietnamese sentence ready: "Tôi cần kiểm tra container số 7 ngay bây giờ." (I need to check container 7 right now.)
